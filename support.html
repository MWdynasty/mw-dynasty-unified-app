MW Dynasty Support

Need help with the MW Athlete app? Send a support request below. You can also use Settings → Help & Account → Report a Problem while signed in.

Common help

Sign-in: Use the email tied to your MW Athlete account. Use Forgot Password on the sign-in screen if needed.

Program week: Your official MW week is controlled by your account's program state. Future weeks stay locked until progression requirements are met or an authorized coach/admin changes your state.

Account deletion: Sign in, open Settings → Help & Account, then choose Delete Account. If you cannot access your account, use the form below and choose Account.
