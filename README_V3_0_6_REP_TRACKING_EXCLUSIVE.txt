MW Dynasty V3.0.6 — Rep Tracking Exclusivity Revision

Product rule:
- Rep-by-rep sprint performance tracking is exclusive to the MW Sprint Performance System.
- Eligible athletes: direct active MW Athlete members, or athletes actively assigned to a coach with MW Sprint Performance access.
- Coach Intelligence keeps intelligence/strength workflows but does not receive MW rep-by-rep sprint tracking.
- Coach Core does not receive rep tracking.
- Workout completion remains available without rep tracking.
- Detailed Sprint Pace AI rep logging is locked outside the MW Sprint Performance System.
