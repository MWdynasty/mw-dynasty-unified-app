MW Dynasty Privacy Policy

Effective: September 15, 2026

MW Dynasty ("MW") provides sprint training, strength programming, pacing and distance tools, athlete profiles, coach dashboards, performance tracking, education, and AI-assisted coaching features. This policy explains the information MW uses to operate the athlete and coach experiences.

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as email address, name, role, account status, and coach access tier; athlete profile information such as date of birth for age eligibility, events, personal records, training level, program week, goals, workout completion, pace check-ins, detailed sprint times you choose to log, strength maxes, strength check-ins, Smart Entry responses, readiness information such as whether you report feeling clear, tight, or in pain, and progress data; coach information such as team/group setup, athlete assignments, coach notes, attendance, messages, program information, and coaching activity; support requests; purchase/subscription status; and content you intentionally send to Coach MW, including text and an image you choose to upload or capture.

Launch diagnostics and reliability data

During launch and beta testing, MW may collect limited authenticated technical diagnostics such as app version/build, app area or route, network availability, API status/error codes, non-sensitive error descriptions, recent successful-sync timing, and membership/entitlement state needed to diagnose access problems. MW diagnostics are designed not to record passwords, authentication tokens, request or message bodies, email addresses, Coach MW message content, uploaded photos, or payment-card information. Diagnostic records are used to troubleshoot crashes, failed syncs, purchase activation, access problems, and other reliability issues.

Location and distance measurement

The Distance Marker may request precise device location while you actively use GPS measurement. The current app uses those coordinates on your device to calculate the distance between your start point and current point; MW does not send or store those GPS coordinates on its servers. MW does not require continuous background location tracking for normal app use. iOS will ask for permission before location access is granted, and you can change that permission in iPhone Settings.

Microphone, speech, and voice features

Voice input may request microphone and speech-recognition permission so you can speak questions instead of typing them. Coach MW voice playback uses generated audio responses. MW does not use the microphone continuously in the background. iOS will ask for permission before microphone or speech access is granted.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, determine authorized athlete or coach access, deliver the correct training progression, calculate individualized pacing and strength guidance, synchronize athlete and coach views, provide performance intelligence, operate Coach MW, maintain account security, respond to support requests, troubleshoot problems, and improve service reliability.

Coach and athlete connections

When an athlete accepts a coach connection, authorized coaches may view performance and training information necessary to coach that athlete, including program state, PRs, pace check-ins, strength information, workout completion, attendance, and related coaching records. Access is limited by account authorization and database access controls.

AI interactions

Text, images, and relevant training context you intentionally send to Coach MW may be processed by MW's AI service provider so the feature can respond. Performance context may also be provided to Coach Intelligence when an authorized coach reviews an assigned athlete. Coach MW is a sports-performance education feature, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Do not submit unnecessary medical, financial, identity-document, password, or payment-card information.

Service providers

MW uses service providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, application delivery, and AI processing. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service. MW does not sell personal information.

Retention and account deletion

Account and training information is retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the service. Athlete users can permanently delete their account from Settings → Help & Account → Delete Account. Coach users can initiate account deletion from Coach Dashboard → More → Account → Delete Account. Because coach accounts may be connected to team or athlete records that need to be handled safely, coach deletion may be processed as an authenticated deletion request rather than immediate removal. Founder and administrator accounts require administrative transfer or removal. If an Apple-billed auto-renewable subscription is active, deleting an MW account does not by itself cancel Apple billing; the subscription must also be managed through Apple.

Security

MW uses authenticated requests, role-based access, account entitlements, and database row-level security to reduce unauthorized access. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

MW Dynasty Athlete accounts are intended for users age 13 or older. Users under 13 should not create or use an Athlete account. MW uses date of birth for age-eligibility enforcement. If you believe information from a child under 13 was submitted to MW Dynasty, contact MW Support so the account and data can be reviewed and removed as appropriate.

Your choices

You can update supported profile information in the app, choose whether to use optional microphone/speech or location features, change iOS permissions in Settings, submit a privacy or account question through MW Support, and initiate account deletion from inside the applicable athlete or coach experience.

Changes

We may update this policy as MW Dynasty evolves. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy, account, or support questions, use MW Dynasty Support.
