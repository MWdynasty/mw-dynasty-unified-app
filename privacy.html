MW Dynasty Privacy Policy

Effective: September 10, 2026

MW Dynasty ("MW") provides sprint-training, pacing, strength, education, progress-tracking, and AI-assisted coaching tools. This policy explains the information used to provide the MW Athlete experience.

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as email address and first name; athlete profile information such as event preferences and sprint personal records; strength-training maxes; program state, workout completions, pace logs, Smart Entry assessment responses, coach connections, support requests, and content you choose to send to Coach MW.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, deliver the correct MW training week, calculate individualized pace and strength guidance, save progress across devices, provide Coach MW responses, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve the reliability of the service.

Service providers

MW uses service providers for hosting, database/authentication, and AI processing. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service. MW does not sell athlete personal information.

AI interactions

Text or images you intentionally send to Coach MW may be processed by MW's AI service provider so the feature can respond. Do not send information that is unnecessary for your training question.

Retention and deletion

Account and training information is retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the service. Athlete users can permanently delete their MW Athlete account from Settings → Help & Account → Delete Account. Deletion removes the account and associated personal training data that MW is not legally required to retain.

Security

MW uses access controls, authenticated requests, and database row-level security to reduce unauthorized access. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

MW Athlete is not intended for children under 13. A person under 13 should not create an MW Athlete account.

Your choices

You can update profile information in the app, change supported notification/preferences, submit a privacy or account question through MW Support, or delete your account in Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy as MW evolves. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy or account questions, use MW Dynasty Support. Signed-in athletes can also use Settings → Help & Account → Report a Problem.
